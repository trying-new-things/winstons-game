📋 Tactical Rulebook

🎯 Objective: Defeat all 3 boss tiers as a team without getting wiped out.

🏁 Corner 1 (START / GO): Pass or land here to gather a +5 Gold paycheck and instantly revive all fallen co-op teammates to max health!

🏥 Corner 2 (Grand Medic): A guaranteed safe haven tile that completely restores all 5 of your hearts.

🎰 Corner 3 (Lucky Casino): Gives you a choice to roll a second movement dice immediately or pocket a flat +5 Gold bonus.

👹 Corner 4 (Boss Lair): Loop 1 traps you in a mini-boss battle. Subsequent loops deal a crushing 3 Hearts of damage (Dodge only on a raw 6!).

💰 Combat & Economy: Landing on Red tiles awards 1 flat Gold per strike. Spend Gold at Shops to manage your item storage (Max 1 item per player).
